1. By ministry type 按事工类型

Not every church on this list holds a service in Chinese. Some run an English service with live translation. The difference matters if you are deciding where to go on Sunday.

Tap a colour to show only those churches. Tap it again to show them all. 点击图例即可只显示该类教会，再点一次恢复全部。

2. By language 按语言

Mandarin, Cantonese, or both in one service. A church appears once for each language it offers.

Tap a language to show only those churches. Tap it again to show them all. 点击语言即可只显示该语言的教会，再点一次恢复全部。

3. Coverage 覆盖范围

Where the ministry reaches, and where it does not. Each circle is the distance a church is assumed to draw people from — drag the slider to change it. The gaps are the point of this map.

Ministers' personal phone numbers are deliberately not published here — contact the parish office. 贵教会资料有误？请电邮 david@yungs.au，或在 sydacm.github.io 该教会下方按「更新资料」（会自动填入现有资料）。本页不刊登牧者私人电话。
